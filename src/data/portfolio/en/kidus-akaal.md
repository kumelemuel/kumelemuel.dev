## Context

Kidus Akaal is a natural cosmetics and herbal brand. It needed a website that conveyed the same things as its products: calm, plant-based ingredients and a close relationship with the people who use them. It did not need an online store, but a place to get to know the brand, browse its products and get in touch.

## What I built

A Spanish-language website that works as a catalog and a brand showcase:

- **Four product lines**: herbal (infusions, oils and plants), cosmetics, hygiene (plant-based soaps and bars) and wellness.
- **Featured products** on the home page, such as rosemary and laurel oil or green clay soap.
- **About**, **Products** and **Contact** pages, plus a privacy notice.
- A visible notice that the wellness preparations do not replace medical advice.

## Decisions

- **Static site with Astro**: there is no cart and no user accounts, so static HTML gives fast pages that are easy to maintain and cheap to host.
- **Catalog instead of store**: sales happen through contact. That kept the project simple and focused on telling the brand's story.
- **Immersive visual identity**: a full-screen home page with plants, a calligraphic typeface for the brand and warm copy about self-care rather than selling.
- **Basic accessibility**: a skip-to-content link and a mobile-friendly menu.
